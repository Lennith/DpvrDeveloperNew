Screen Casting Guide

This guide provides instructions on how to cast your VR device screen to other displays using Miracast or Wifi casting.

01

Enter Quick Settings

From the home screen, navigate to and enter the Quick Settings menu.

02

Select Screen Cast

Locate and click on the Screen Cast option within the quick settings menu.

03

Choose Casting Method

There are two methods available:

  • Android screen cast: Standard Miracast. Requires a receiving device that supports Miracast.
  • Wireless screen cast: DPVR Wifi casting. Requires TV_ScreenPlayer.apk installed on an Android TV/tablet (v4.4+).
Ensure both the VR device and the receiving device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network for Wireless screen cast.
04

Android Screen Cast Steps

Click the icon in the top right corner and enable the system casting function. Then, select the target Miracast receiving device from the list.

05

Wireless Screen Cast Steps

Open the TV_ScreenPlayer app on your target device and select the Start Screen Casting button to start casting.
