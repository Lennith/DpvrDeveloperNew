ConnectWifiDisplay#

Connect to WifiDisplay, you can connect based on Mac or device name. The priority of the Mac address is higher than the device name. In the entire DMS task queue, only the last call of ConnectWifiDisplay is retained. The connection is monitored through RegisterConnectionCallback, and calling this interface will automatically turn on the WifiDisplay function.
