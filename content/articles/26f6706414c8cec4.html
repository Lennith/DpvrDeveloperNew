Local Upgrade Guide

Follow these steps to update your device firmware.

01

Connect and Copy Upgrade File

Power on the device and connect it to your computer using a USB data cable. Locate the device storage on your computer, then copy the upgrade package update.zip to the **root directory** of the internal shared storage.

(Cannot find the device disk? Check Troubleshoot: Disk Not Showing)

Note: Please copy the ZIP package directly; **do not unzip** the file.
update.zip" class="step-image">
02

Enter System Settings

Start the device, find and click the Settings icon on the home screen.

03

Select Firmware Menu

After entering settings, find and click Firmware Version in the left list.

04

Execute Local Upgrade

On the System Update screen, click the Local Upgrade button on the right.

Battery Warning: Before operation, ensure the device battery level is greater than **40%** to prevent system damage from power interruption during the upgrade.
05

Automatic Restart

The system will automatically verify the upgrade package and install it. After installation is complete, the device will **automatically restart**.

Please wait patiently and do not forcibly shut down or remove power during this time.
