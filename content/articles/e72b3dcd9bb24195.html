Release Note¶

2025-05-27¶

  1. Added support for updating the authorization date during video encryption. If not specified, the original authorization date will be used.

  2. Added support for omitting output_vei_key when updating video encryption. The previously used VEI_KEY will be retained.

  3. Fixed several documentation issues.
